# Film metadata and streaming availability

## Primary provider

Movie of the Night supplies film and television metadata and country-specific streaming offers. A direct developer key enables the live catalogue without TMDB. Browse, title search, weekly picks, dice and wheel require a current base-subscription offer from a selected service in the viewing country. Choose subscriptions separately for the UK, Ireland, US, Canada or Australia. The listings use the provider's first result page, so the weekly shelf may have fewer than five films and one series. Series offers can vary by season. Movie runtimes and release years are shown when supplied. [Movie of the Night API documentation](https://docs.movieofthenight.com/resource/shows)

Title details distinguish subscriptions, paid channels, rental, purchase and free offers. The app links to the service when the provider supplies a valid HTTPS link and shows when it checked the offers. Missing coverage remains unverified. The provider's direct terms permit commercial use of covered data in applications and restrict redistribution as a standalone data product. Third-party artwork, descriptions and brand materials carry separate rights. Movie of the Night artwork remains disabled. Review commercial clearance and third-party content rights before launch. [Direct developer terms](https://developers.movieofthenight.com/terms-and-conditions)

TMDB is optional and serves legacy saved-title details when Movie of the Night is disabled. Leave `CATALOGUE_USE` and `CATALOGUE_API_TOKEN` blank unless you need that fallback and qualify for its non-commercial developer access. The owner received a US$149/month commercial quote and declined it. TMDB artwork needs separate clearance before enabling `CATALOGUE_POSTERS_ALLOWED=yes`. The TMDB mark in Credits is the official short logo. [TMDB licensing](https://developer.themoviedb.org/docs/faq) · [Attribution rules](https://www.themoviedb.org/about/logos-attribution)

## Enable the direct free plan locally

1. Sign up on the Movie of the Night developer portal. Choose the direct free plan and confirm the quota in your dashboard. The direct free plan can be opened without payment details. [Developer portal](https://developers.movieofthenight.com/) · [Authentication guide](https://docs.movieofthenight.com/guide/authentication)
2. Copy `.env.example` to `.env.local` if it does not already exist. Put the key in `AVAILABILITY_API_TOKEN` and set `AVAILABILITY_FREE_PLAN=yes` and `AVAILABILITY_TERMS_ACCEPTED=yes` after confirming the plan and terms. Set `AVAILABILITY_FREE_REQUEST_LIMIT=450` if the account quota supports it, or set a smaller positive number within the account quota. Keep the key out of chat, Git and browser code.
3. Restart with `npm.cmd run dev`. Open [the local site](http://127.0.0.1:5173/) and look for **Live catalogue**. In Profile, edit viewing preferences and select the country and services you already subscribe to. Enter a title and submit the search or open a weekly pick to see the offers.

## Request budget and caching

- The app enforces at most **450 upstream requests per rolling 31 days**, or a lower configured limit. A local ignored ledger survives restarts, and the server reserves a slot before each upstream attempt. The provider account quota is separate. The direct terms say API access pauses at the plan cap without request overage fees. This local ledger covers this checkout only. Keep the provider account on the free plan. [Direct developer terms](https://developers.movieofthenight.com/terms-and-conditions)
- Requests are server-side, with an eight-second timeout, a separate short-term throttle and a cooldown when the provider rate limits requests. Simultaneous matching requests share one fetch. Completed Movie of the Night responses are cached in memory for `AVAILABILITY_CACHE_MINUTES` (0 by default, at most 360). Configure a positive value only within the applicable terms. Active catalogue results stop qualifying after six hours or a known offer expiry. [Direct developer terms](https://developers.movieofthenight.com/terms-and-conditions)
- The local Vite dev and preview servers handle the API. The production build is static. A hosted live catalogue would need equivalent server routing, protected secrets and a shared quota mechanism.

## Supplementary sources evaluated

- **Wikidata** publishes structured data under CC0. IMDb identifiers and duration statements could supplement individual matched titles. Matching by a verified identifier and checking duration units and editions would be necessary. This review did not measure completeness or accuracy. Wikidata does not supply the verified country and subscription offers needed here. Commons media carries file-specific reuse terms. [Wikidata licensing](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Wikidata:Licensing) · [IMDb identifier](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Property:P345) · [Duration property](https://www.wikidata.org/wiki/Property:P2047) · [Commons reuse](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Commons:Reusing_content_outside_Wikimedia)
- **TVmaze** provides a free public API for shows, seasons, episodes and schedules, with IMDb or TheTVDB lookup for matching. Its API data requires attribution and ShareAlike compliance under CC BY-SA. It could supplement TV metadata, but its network and streaming schedules cannot establish current country-specific subscription, rental, purchase or channel entitlements. It does not fill the general film catalogue. Artwork still requires a separate rights check. [TVmaze API and licence](https://www.tvmaze.com/api) · [TVmaze copyright policy](https://www.tvmaze.com/site/copyright)

These were documentation reviews. Neither supplementary source is integrated, and neither is used to infer streaming eligibility.
